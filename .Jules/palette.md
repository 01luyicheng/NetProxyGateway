## 2024-08-08 - Disabled States Contrast in Jetpack Compose
**Learning:** When a Compose Button is disabled, its text content color automatically adjusts for low contrast (e.g., gray out), but a `CircularProgressIndicator` inside it will not inherit this unless explicitly styled with `LocalContentColor.current`. Hardcoding colors like `onPrimary` on a loading spinner causes severe contrast issues (invisible on light disabled backgrounds) during async disabled states.
**Action:** Always use `LocalContentColor.current` for spinners inside Buttons, and wrap dynamic button content (like loading states) in `AnimatedContent` (along with a `Row` to restore the scope) to prevent abrupt layout jumping and guarantee correct disabled contrast transitions.
